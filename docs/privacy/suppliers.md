## Other paths and exclusions

- **Google Fonts:** removed by self-hosting in T001/PR #48; `frontend/index.html` and `frontend/src/styles/tokens.css` use local font files. T001 records a browser-network check. Google is therefore removed from the current recipient list; repeat the browser request check before publication if the frontend changes.
- **GitHub/GHCR:** holds source, build artifacts and CI logs. The repository is designed to use synthetic/test database data, not production account or rights-request records. Verify CI logs and any support issues before treating this as a permanent exclusion. Do not post personal requests or evidence here.
- **The user's device:** the browser stores a JWT in `localStorage`; a downloaded export is user-controlled after delivery. Neither is a supplier receiving a service-side copy. Browser storage and disposal appear in [retention.md](retention.md).
- **Operator's restricted local register:** Q8 places only minimal request metadata on a FileVault-encrypted local device with cloud sync disabled. No new hosted register supplier is introduced. Verify backup/sync settings before the first request.
- **Other support recipients/CDNs:** none identified from the current source and network path. This is a discovery result, not a guarantee about unreviewed provider support tickets, diagnostics or future integrations. Any support disclosure of user material must be inventoried before it occurs.

### T076 agreement review, 2026-09-29

Account types came from read-only CLI checks (Azure, Neon) and the owner (Cloudflare). The terms were read from the providers' public pages on this date. Nothing needed signing: each agreement below applies through the terms the account already accepted.

- **Azure.** The billing account is an **individual Microsoft Customer Agreement**, Pay-As-You-Go, active. The [Product Terms' privacy and security terms](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/PrivacyandSecurityTerms) make the [Microsoft Products and Services DPA](https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/docs/view/Microsoft-Products-and-Services-Data-Protection-Addendum-DPA?lang=1) (May 2026 edition) govern Customer Data. Container Apps and Log Analytics aren't among its listed product exceptions. Microsoft is the processor. The DPA's own text is a download that wasn't read here, so its transfer and post-termination clauses are recorded by reference only.
- **Neon / Databricks.** The organization is on the **Free plan**. The [Neon schedule](https://neon.com/platform-terms) (2026-08-05) covers the Neon product family and sits under the [Databricks MCSA](https://www.databricks.com/legal/mcsa). The MCSA incorporates the then-current DPA by reference (§1.1.4.1), and the schedule amends that DPA's audit section, so no separate signature is needed; e-signing is optional. The owner supplied Databricks' sample DPA **v3 (2023-07-21)**. It may predate the current online version, and later updates may not materially reduce protection (§11.1). It provides:
  - Databricks acts as **processor** (§2.1). Special categories, including health data, may be included at the customer's discretion (Annex 1(B)).
  - Subprocessors under general authorization, with 30 days' notice of new ones to people subscribed to [the list](https://www.databricks.com/legal/databricks-subprocessors) (§4.1–4.4). The owner can subscribe there for notices.
  - Reasonable cooperation with data-subject requests the customer can't handle through the service's own controls (§5.1). Breach notice within 72 hours (§6.2).
  - Restricted transfers under the **EU SCCs, Modules 2/3**, governed by Irish law (§8.1, Annex B). A certificate of deletion is available on written request (Annex B 2(b)).
  - Deletion when the customer uses the service's controls (§9.2), and within **30 days of a written request** after termination (§9.3).
  - §9.1 says the Databricks services include no backups, while the Neon schedule says Neon databases are backed up. The Neon-specific schedule is taken to govern Neon. Neither document gives a timetable for deleting Neon's internal durability copies.
- **Cloudflare.** The zone is on the **Free plan** (owner-confirmed). The [Self-Serve Subscription Agreement](https://www.cloudflare.com/terms/) (updated 2025-09-12) covers free services and incorporates the [Customer DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) (v6.4, 2026-04-03) by reference (§6.1). That DPA makes Cloudflare a processor, uses the EU SCCs and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and deletes or returns personal data after the agreement ends. It doesn't specifically address DNS query metadata, which usually comes from resolvers rather than visitors. Cloudflare only serves DNS here; it doesn't proxy page or API traffic.

**Residual unknown, owner decision 2026-09-29.** No provider publishes when its internal copies (backups, durability or replication copies) of deleted data are physically erased. The owner accepted this as a residual risk instead of asking each provider's support for a written timeline. The settings the owner controls set the effective limits: Neon's six-hour history, Azure's 30-day retention with `immediatePurgeDataOn30Days`, and the app's own deletion. Beyond them, the reliance is each DPA's deletion commitment. The final notice must describe provider copies within those limits and must not promise a provider-side erasure date. T077 still observes the limits the owner controls. Recheck when a provider's terms change materially, a plan changes (for example Neon Free to paid, or Cloudflare proxying turned on), or a new supplier is added.
